# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Mexican student-athletes (primarily swimmers, 15-22 years old) who are actively pursuing NCAA college scholarship opportunities. They use the app during training cycles and recruiting seasons — on their phones between practices, on laptops when researching universities at night. Their primary job: turn the chaos of recruiting into a clear, organized path toward a US college scholarship. They feel overwhelmed by the volume of information, emails, timelines, and decisions they must track.

## Product Purpose

Ximo is a paid subscription app that organizes the full college recruiting journey for Mexican athletes. It centralizes university research, coach communication, athletic progress, academic test prep, documents, tasks, and brand opportunities into a single dark command-center interface. Success is a Mexican athlete arriving at the right US university with the right scholarship because they stayed organized and moved with confidence.

## Brand Personality

Cinematic, disciplined, ambitious. The brand speaks with confidence — not hype. It feels like a professional command center, not a school app.

## Anti-references

- Generic white SaaS dashboards (Notion, Airtable light mode)
- School project aesthetics (Canva templates, colorful emoji-heavy UIs)
- Cheap sports apps (cluttered, bright, loud)
- AI design factory outputs: cream backgrounds, pill-badge everything, 3-column identical card grids, uppercase eyebrows on every section

## Design Principles

1. **Command center, not checklist app.** Every screen should feel like the athlete is in control of a complex operation, not filling out a form. Density earns its place; empty space is intentional authority.
2. **Dark by default, readable always.** The dark teal/navy/gold system is the product identity. Contrast is non-negotiable — if a heading blends into the background, it does not exist.
3. **Motion as confirmation, not decoration.** Animations signal that the system is working. Transitions confirm navigation. Nothing moves without purpose.
4. **Subscription gates quality.** The product has no free tier. Subscription status is visible and real — the app communicates active access clearly.
5. **Mexico-first confidence.** The product is built for Mexican athletes who want to compete at the highest level. The voice is direct, not cautious. The design should feel like it belongs on a D1 training room wall.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG AA minimum for text contrast on dark backgrounds. Reduced-motion support via `prefers-reduced-motion`. Spanish-language primary UI. Tabular numerals for stats and times.
